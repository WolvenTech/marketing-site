**Offer (all four lines, per the Human):** software development and engineering, AI and automation consulting, product and design studio, managed services and ops. The Human added that Wolven does not do brand or infra work, so those score "a max of 10". What the 10 is out of is unclear; ticket 11 settles it.

**Buyer (hypothesis, not validated):** startups and mid-market companies, mainly in the US. Enterprise in Brazil is the runner-up. The Human says the final buyer profile has to be discovered with probes, so no single ICP is chosen yet. The PMM personas (economic buyer, technical buyer, user/champion) are the working frame for each segment.

**Evidence:** 5+ clients exist and the Human can share data. That data is not in this session, so the PMM validation (top 20% by lifetime value, fastest sales cycles, lowest churn) has not run. Ticket 12 does it.

**Talent for the recruiting layer:** software engineers only.

**What the site must do for each audience:** not settled here. It follows from the ICP (ticket 12), the probes (ticket 13) and positioning (ticket 05).
